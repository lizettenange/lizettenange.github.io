 Thanks to my advisor, I had the privilege of participating in the International Conference on Soft Materials (ICSM-2024), which was organized by Rovira i Virgili University and the Soft Materials Research Society (SMRS). The event took place at Universitat Rovira i Virgili from November 17th to 20th, 2024.

During the conference, I had the opportunity to engage with researchers from India, Spain, and France. These interactions deepened my understanding of their work and provided valuable insights into the diverse applications of soft materials in fields like biology and gas sensing.

In addition to expanding my knowledge, I also learned valuable skills for presenting at conferences. Some of my key takeaways included the importance of calm delivery, creating engaging introductions, and focusing on simplicity over complexity. Overall, attending ICSM-2024 was an enriching experience that enhanced both my academic and professional growth, and I look forward to applying these lessons moving forward.
