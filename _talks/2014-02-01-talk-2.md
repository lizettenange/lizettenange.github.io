I was honoured to receive the award for Best Oral Presentation at the Symposium for Women in STEM, organised by the Faculty of Sciences at the University of Dschang, Cameroon. The prize was presented at the award ceremony organised by the Faculty.

This recognition is not just a personal achievement, but a testament to the fact that with hard work and perseverance, success is within reach, regardless of gender. It adds to the growing list of accomplishments I have been fortunate to achieve as a PhD student.

We continue to work hard and remain hopeful for more and better opportunities to uplift and valorise women in STEM.
